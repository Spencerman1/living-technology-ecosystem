Layer 6 — Security & Legal Layer 

Integrity, authentication, and legal enforceability for the Mint-to Logic™ ecosystem

1 Purpose

Layer 6 provides the protective and evidentiary envelope for the infrastructure.
Its design goal is twofold:

Integrity Control – every reflex action is cryptographically authenticated and resistant to tampering.

Legal Recognition – every recorded event can be presented as admissible proof under U.S. and international frameworks.

Evidence Bundle → CSML hash chain + timestamp package created.

Notice Phase → Cease-and-Desist or DMCA takedown issued with forensic proof.
